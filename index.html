Are you big enough for my project?

We’re intentionally small—that means faster decisions and direct access to builders. For large multi-team programs we’ll be upfront if a partner network is a better fit.

Can you sign an NDA before we share details?

Yes. We’re happy to sign mutual NDAs before discussing sensitive product plans or data.

What does engagement cost?

It depends on scope. After discovery we quote fixed milestones or monthly sprint blocks. Founding partners often start with a focused MVP or audit engagement.
